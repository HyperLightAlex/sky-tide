the title card and the eyecatch. Play is crisp; these moments carry the broadcast touch
(4:3 safe framing, a little softness and bleed).

| Sheet | What | How the game uses it |
|---|---|---|
| K1 Key art | The rider riding a stream at golden hour, the wake rising behind, islands and cumulus around: the cover | The cover and loading screen; the reference for every later sheet |

## Prompt

```
SKYTIDE: KEY SHEETS (three sheets; make them one at a time, in order, each as its own image; attach the rider and board model sheets)

STYLE, ALL SHEETS
- Key art from a Japanese TV anime of around 2005, made in digital ink and paint: the characters in clean, steady, thin dark lines with flat cel colour and one hard shadow shape; backgrounds painted by hand; light effects composited as glows.
- The trapar (the current the board rides) is drawn two ways: the streams themselves as translucent cerulean blue bands with thin white glowing edge lines; the wave a board throws up as glowing lime green #7CF22A with a gold-white core, a sea-glass teal body, thick white glowing crest lines and white water dashes.
- Not a photo, not a 3D render, not a modern painterly illustration: no lens flare, no depth-of-field blur, no film grain.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere.

SHEET K1: KEY ART (1024x1536, portrait, full bleed)
- The rider (attached) carving hard on the board (attached) across a translucent blue stream at golden hour, seen from slightly below and to the side, the wave of green light and white water rising behind and curling over, spray flying.
- Behind: towering cumulus lit gold on top with slate shadow sides, a floating island with a waterfall spilling off it, more streams sweeping through the sky in long curves.
- Deep blue sky above, warm gold low on the horizon.

SHEET K2: TITLE CARD (1440x1080, 4:3, full bleed)
- A calm wide view: a sea of cloud far below at golden hour, a single translucent blue stream curving away into the distance, a far floating island small on the horizon, the sky deep blue at the top.
- Leave the upper middle third clear and plain (the game sets the title there).

SHEET K3: EYECATCH (1536x1024, landscape: two 768x1024 panels side by side, each its own picture)
- Left: the rider sitting on the edge of a floating island's dock, board beside them, looking out at the clouds, painted simply against a flat bright sky-blue ground with a white border (an eyecatch card).
- Right: the board alone, floating in the air with a little green glow under it and a few white flecks, against a flat warm gold ground with a white border.
- Leave the bottom fifth of each panel clear (the game sets a caption there).
```
