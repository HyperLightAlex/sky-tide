# Model sheets

The rider and the board, designed first as concept sheets (pick one), then drawn as model
sheets (four views at one scale) to build the 3D models from. The placeholders in
`render/riderView.js` stand in until then. Both designs are **original**: the look
references are for colour, line and motion only.

| Sheet | What | How the game uses it |
|---|---|---|
| D1 Rider concepts | Six rider designs side by side, full figure, front three-quarter, on green | To choose a design (no game use) |
| D2 Board concepts | Six board designs, top and side, on green | To choose a design (no game use) |
| M1 Rider model sheet | The chosen rider: front, side, back, three-quarter, at one scale, with a colour key | The 3D model is built from it; its colours become the cel paints (each with its own shadow colour) |
| M2 Board model sheet | The chosen board: top, bottom, side, front, three-quarter, at one scale, with a colour key | As M1 |
| M3 Rider expressions and hands | Six heads (expressions) and six hand poses | Close-up shots and title cards later |

Attach to D1 and D2: the stills of the rider in the air and on the board (for the era's line
and colour only). Attach to M1 and M2: the chosen concept panel.

## D1 Rider concepts

```
SKYTIDE: RIDER CONCEPTS (one sheet)

Character designs for the hero of a game about surfing streams of light high in the sky on a board: a teenager (about 15) who rides for the joy of it, light and quick, at home in the wind. Original designs: do not copy or resemble any existing anime or game character.

STYLE
- Character design from a Japanese TV anime of around 2005, made in digital ink and paint: clean, steady, thin dark lines of even weight, flat cel colour with one hard shadow shape per form in a cooler, muted shade of each colour, a small highlight on hair and glossy parts.
- Natural proportions (about 6.5 heads tall), expressive but not exaggerated; a clear silhouette readable small on screen.
- Not a 3D render, not a modern painterly illustration: no gradients on the cels, no texture, no grain.

THE SHEET
- 1536x1024, landscape, on perfectly flat pure green #00FF00 (no shading, texture, gradient or shadow on the green; nothing in the designs green or close to it).
- Six different designs in a row, each a full figure standing in a relaxed three-quarter front pose, the same height, not touching.
- Each wears practical gear for riding fast in cold high air: a light jacket or shell, something to protect the eyes (goggles, a visor, glasses), gloves or wraps, shoes that grip a board. Vary them: sporty, scruffy, neat, layered, minimal, a bit of a mechanic.
- Each design has a strong two- or three-colour scheme that stands out against blue sky and white cloud, and against green light (avoid green outfits). One accent colour each.
- No board, no text, no numbers, no logos, no labels, no signatures or watermarks.
```

## D2 Board concepts

```
SKYTIDE: BOARD CONCEPTS (one sheet)

Designs for a board that rides streams of light in the sky (a "lifting" board: part surfboard, part snowboard, light enough to carry, with a hard edge for carving and somewhere on its underside that catches the glowing current). Original designs: do not copy or resemble any existing anime or game vehicle or board.

STYLE
- Prop design from a Japanese TV anime of around 2005, made in digital ink and paint: clean, steady, thin dark lines; flat cel colour with one hard shadow shape and a crisp highlight on glossy parts.
- Not a 3D render, not a modern painterly illustration.

THE SHEET
- 1536x1024, landscape, on perfectly flat pure green #00FF00 (no shading, texture, gradient or shadow on the green; nothing in the designs green or close to it).
- A 3x2 grid, one design per cell, each shown twice: from the top (nose up) and from the side below it, at one scale, about 2 m long.
- Vary them: a slim pintail, a wide stubby freestyle board, a twin-tip, one with a raised nose kick, one with fins or rails that catch the current, one with a split tail.
- Each with a clear two-colour scheme, its underside a darker colour with the current-catching part marked out in a light colour (the game makes it glow green when riding).
- No text, numbers, logos, labels, signatures or watermarks.
```

## M1 Rider model sheet (after D1 is chosen)

```
SKYTIDE: RIDER MODEL SHEET (one sheet; attach the chosen concept)

A model sheet of the attached rider design, to build a 3D model from.

STYLE: as the concept sheet: TV anime of around 2005, clean thin even lines, flat cel colour with one hard shadow shape per form in a cooler, muted shade of each colour.

THE SHEET
- 1536x1024, landscape, on perfectly flat pure green #00FF00.
- Four views of the same figure at exactly the same scale, standing straight with arms slightly away from the body (a relaxed A-pose), feet level on one line: front, side (facing left), back, and front three-quarter.
- A colour key along the bottom: a swatch of each colour with its shadow colour beside it.
- Every detail consistent between the views (seams, pockets, straps, hair locks).
- No board, no text, logos, signatures or watermarks.
```
